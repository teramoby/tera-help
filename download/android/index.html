Updating tt and Handling a Signature Conflict

Before installing a newer direct APK, compare the version published here with the version shown for tt in Android's app information. An accepted update normally installs over the existing app and retains its app data. Do not assume an APK with a higher filename or a copy from another site is an official update.

If Android refuses the update or reports that the package conflicts with an existing package, the signing identity or another package property may not match. Do not immediately uninstall the existing copy. This page cannot confirm that uninstalling and reinstalling will preserve your local data.

Before you uninstall an existing copy

  • Conversations and settings are stored locally as Android app data and may be removed when the app is uninstalled.
  • Encrypted API keys are excluded from Android system backup; do not expect a device backup to restore those keys.
  • Make sure you can access the relevant provider accounts and create or enter replacement credentials if needed.
  • If a key may have been exposed, revoke or rotate it in the provider's own console.

Read the Privacy Policy and BYOK privacy guide before making a decision about app data or credentials. Neither the APK checksum nor an Android backup is a substitute for keeping control of your provider accounts.

What You Need After Installation

tt uses bring your own key (BYOK). The download does not include an AI subscription, credits, or shared access to an AI provider. To start a chat, you need a supported provider or hosting-platform account, a complete model selection, and the credential required by that service. Provider availability, billing, quotas, model access, and data terms remain part of your own provider account.

  1. Open tt and go to Settings → Model Configuration.
  2. Choose a service available to your account, then enter the credential that service requires.
  3. Select a model and save the configuration. Configure one complete service for a single-model chat or several for a multi-model workflow.
Only enter a provider key in the intended configuration field. Never paste an API key into a chat message, a search box, or an untrusted webpage. Use provider-side budgets, quotas, and scoped credentials when available.
