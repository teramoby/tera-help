Set up your own AI providers with BYOK

tt is a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) AI client. It does not include an AI subscription or resell provider usage. To start a conversation, you need credentials and available API access for at least one supported AI provider or managed platform. Consumer chat subscriptions and developer API access are often separate products.

Configure one service

Open Settings → Model Configuration, choose a service, enter the credential it requires, select a model, and save. One complete configuration is enough for a single-model conversation.

Configure several services

Add multiple complete configurations when you want side-by-side answers, a synthesized result, or a shared Team Mode discussion. Billing, quotas, model access, and regional limits remain with each provider account.

Enter provider credentials only inside tt's settings or the provider's own service. Review the BYOK and local privacy guide before connecting an account used for sensitive work.

Compare several AI models on iPhone and iPad

Standard chat is useful when you want a quick response, independent answers from several configured services, or a synthesized result. A fair comparison starts with the same prompt, the same requested output format, and clear evaluation criteria. Different answers are evidence to investigate—not proof that the majority is correct.

The AI model comparison guide includes a reusable prompt, a scoring rubric, fact-checking steps, and a method for separating agreement from repeated error. It can help you compare reasoning, coverage, uncertainty, and source quality without inventing a benchmark result.

Use Team Mode for a shared discussion

Team Mode gives all currently configured services with a complete model and credential a shared conversation. Participating models can respond to you and one another, challenge assumptions, add missing context, or remain quiet when they have nothing new to contribute. An @ mention asks one model to respond first without excluding the others.

Use standard chat for fast comparison or synthesis. Use Team Mode when a question benefits from critique, trade-off analysis, or several models building on prior messages. Neither workflow guarantees factual accuracy; verify important medical, legal, financial, and other consequential claims against reliable sources.

Privacy on iPhone and iPad

  • Local app data: conversations, provider configurations, and preferences are stored as app data on your device. tt does not provide a teramoby account or a teramoby-operated cloud synchronization service.
  • System services: eligible local app data may be handled by operating-system backup or device migration according to your platform and account settings.
  • Model requests: your message and the context required for the selected workflow are sent directly to the AI services you configure. BYOK does not make cloud models operate offline.
  • Shared context: later requests in multi-model synthesis or Team Mode can include answers or discussion context produced by another provider.
  • Online search: the current Apple implementation performs a search for non-empty prompts while online search is enabled. You can disable online search in settings.

These points are a practical summary, not a replacement for the full Privacy Policy. Decide which services are appropriate for your data and also review the policies of every provider you connect.
