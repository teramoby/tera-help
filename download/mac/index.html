Use one model or several

Standard chat

With one configured service, tt can show that model’s response directly. With several complete configurations, standard chat can collect model answers and use the selected summarizer to organize them into one result.

Team Mode

Team Mode gives participating models a shared discussion. They can add context, challenge an assumption, respond to earlier messages, or stay quiet when they have nothing useful to add.

These modes suit different tasks. Standard chat is useful for a quick response, side-by-side material, or a synthesized answer. Team Mode is designed for questions that benefit from critique, trade-off analysis, and several models building on one another.

Compare model answers responsibly

Multiple answers can reveal disagreements, different assumptions, or missing considerations, but agreement between models is not proof. Models can share the same error or reinforce an incorrect premise. For a fair comparison, use the same prompt, context, output constraints, and evaluation criteria across models.

The AI model comparison guide provides a repeatable workflow for evaluating relevance, factual support, reasoning, clarity, and uncertainty without inventing a universal “best” model. Verify consequential medical, legal, financial, security, or other high-stakes claims against reliable sources.

Privacy on your Mac

Conversations, provider configurations, and preferences are stored as app data on your device. Provider credentials are stored in the device’s secure system storage rather than ordinary files as plain text. When you request an answer, the necessary prompt and context still go to the AI services you selected under their own privacy and retention policies.

Multi-model synthesis can send completed model replies to the synthesizing service. Team Mode can include earlier shared discussion context in later requests. When Online Search is active, a concise query derived from your prompt may also be sent through the search flow described in the Privacy Policy. Read the plain-language BYOK guide before using sensitive information.

Mac download FAQ

Does tt support Apple Silicon and Intel Macs?

Yes. The current direct Mac package is published as a Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. It requires macOS 14.0 or later.

Is tt free to download?

tt is free to download and use. AI provider API calls are billed separately by the providers you choose, according to their own pricing and account rules.

Can I use tt with only one AI model?

Yes. One complete provider and model configuration is enough to begin a single-model chat. Configure several services for comparison, synthesis, or Team Mode.

Does BYOK mean tt works fully offline?

No. BYOK means you control the provider accounts and credentials. Cloud models still need the prompt and required context to be sent to their services over the network.

What should I do if the Mac App Store listing is unavailable?

App Store availability can vary by region and change over time. You can review the official direct DMG on this page, provided your Mac meets the stated requirements. Do not use unofficial download mirrors.
