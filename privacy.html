Privacy Policy

tt by teramoby (referred to below as “tt”) stores nothing about you. All data lives on your device, and our developers have no technical means to access it — ever.

Data Storage

Stored Locally on Your Device

Everything you create in tt — conversations, configurations, preferences — is stored exclusively on your device. There is no cloud sync, no remote backup, and no server-side storage of any kind.

We Collect Zero Data

tt does not collect, log, or transmit any user data — including conversation content and history, usage behavior and analytics, device information and identifiers, location data, or any personally identifiable information (PII).

Data Security

Developers Cannot Access Your Data

tt has no backend servers, data pipelines, or telemetry systems. Even if we wanted to access your data, it would be technically impossible. Your privacy is protected at the architectural level — not just by policy.

API Key Security

API keys you configure for third-party AI providers are stored only in your device's secure system storage. They are never transmitted to tt's developers and are never used for any other purpose.

Third-Party Services

Third-Party AI Providers

tt's core function is to transmit the text you type into the conversation interface (your messages and prompts) directly from your device to the AI provider you choose and configure (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, etc.) in order to generate AI responses. This transmission uses your own API key to connect directly with the provider. tt does not intercept, store, or process this content in any way.

This data transmission is entirely user-initiated: you choose which provider to use, configure your own API key, and decide what to send in each conversation. You retain full control over all data flows. Your conversation content is used solely to request responses from your chosen provider and for no other purpose.

Each AI provider processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy, independently of tt. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of your chosen provider before use.
