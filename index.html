Privacy

Local-first, with a clear data flow

Stored Locally on Your Device

Your conversation history and settings are stored as app data on your device. Your operating system may include this data in system backup or device transfer.

No teramoby Analytics

tt does not send full conversation history, usage analytics, device identifiers, or personal identifiers to teramoby for product analytics.

No teramoby Account or Cloud

tt does not use a teramoby account, app cloud-sync service, data pipeline, or telemetry service.

API Key Security

Third-party API keys are kept in your device's secure system storage and used only to authenticate with the service you configure. They are not sent to teramoby.

Model requests travel directly from your device to the configured AI providers. When Online Search is active and a search is needed, a derived query is sent to teramoby's Cloudflare-hosted search endpoint. See the Privacy Policy for backup, caching, and third-party processing details.
