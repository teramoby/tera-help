Introducing tt by teramoby

A multi-model AI client for Android, iPhone, iPad, and Mac that can compare and synthesize replies in standard chat or let models discuss in Team Mode.

teramoby today announced the general availability of tt by teramoby, a multi-model AI client for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android that enables users to query multiple large language models simultaneously and receive a single, synthesized answer.

Most AI applications today rely on a single model, yet different models frequently produce divergent — and sometimes contradictory — responses to the same question. tt addresses this by querying multiple leading providers in parallel, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, then collecting all responses and sending them to a designated model for synthesis. The result is a final answer that draws on multiple independent perspectives.

Read the complete list of supported model providers and managed platforms, or see how models can respond to one another in Team Mode.

One model gives you one perspective. tt puts several perspectives in the same conversation, where you can inspect both agreement and disagreement.

tt operates on a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) model. Users supply their own credentials, and model requests go directly from the device to the providers they configure. tt does not upload full conversation history or credentials to teramoby for account sync or analytics; Online Search has a separate data flow documented in the Privacy Policy.

Pricing and Availability

tt by teramoby is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android. The App Store listing is named “teramoby tera (tt)” and is not currently available in the mainland China storefront. Users supply their own API keys from supported AI providers.

Getting Started

Download from the App Store

Open the direct App Store link on this site, or search for teramoby and download “teramoby tera (tt)” for free on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Configure Your API Keys

Open tt and go to Settings → Model Configuration. Select the AI providers you want to use (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic), paste in your API keys, and save. Keys are stored securely in your system's secure storage.

Ask Your Question

Type your question and send. tt sends it in parallel to every service whose model and credential configuration is complete.

Review the Synthesized Answer

tt collects completed responses and sends them to your chosen summarizer, which organizes the common points and differences into one answer. Important claims should still be verified.
