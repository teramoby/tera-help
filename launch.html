Introducing tt by teramoby

A multi-model AI client for Android, iPhone, iPad, and Mac that lets models discuss a question and synthesizes their perspectives into one answer.

teramoby today announced the general availability of tt by teramoby, a multi-model AI client for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android that enables users to query multiple large language models simultaneously and receive a single, synthesized answer.

Most AI applications today rely on a single model, yet different models frequently produce divergent — and sometimes contradictory — responses to the same question. tt addresses this by querying multiple leading providers in parallel, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, then collecting all responses and sending them to a designated model for synthesis. The result is a final answer that draws on multiple independent perspectives.

The most reliable answer doesn't come from the best single model — it comes from bringing multiple independent perspectives together. tt makes that accessible to everyone.

tt operates on a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) model. Users supply their own API keys for each AI provider, and all conversation data flows directly between the user's device and the provider. No data passes through teramoby's servers, ensuring privacy at the architectural level.

Pricing and Availability

tt by teramoby is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android. The App Store listing is named “teramoby tera (tt).” Users supply their own API keys from supported AI providers.

Getting Started

Download from the App Store

Open the direct App Store link on this site, or search for teramoby and download “teramoby tera (tt)” for free on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Configure Your API Keys

Open tt and go to Settings → Model Configuration. Select the AI providers you want to use (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic), paste in your API keys, and save. Keys are stored securely in your system's secure storage.

Choose Models & Ask

On the main screen, select the models you want to query at the same time. Type your question and send — tt will fire off requests to all selected models in parallel.

Get a Synthesized Answer

tt collects all responses and sends them to a single model, which synthesizes them into one objective, reliable final answer.
